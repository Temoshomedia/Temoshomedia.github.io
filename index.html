How it works

Here's exactly what happens, quarter by quarter

We take the content you send us and turn it into ads that bring in consultations — no guesswork, no back-and-forth on strategy.

Quick clips and photos of your day-to-day work — nothing staged. Drop at least 12 pieces every quarter into a shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder. No sorting or trimming required.
