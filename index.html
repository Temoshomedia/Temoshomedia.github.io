FAQ

Good questions.

Will it sound like a robot?

We build it around your business and your approved information, and we review its replies with you before it goes live. It is meant to sound like your team on a good day.

What happens with complicated questions?

Complex or sensitive enquiries are escalated to your staff, so a person handles anything that needs human attention.

What does the setup fee cover?

The building: learning your business, setting up your workflows, connecting your channels and tools, and testing it with you before launch.

Which tools does it connect to?

That depends on your setup. Integrations may include email platforms, website chat, telephony, calendars, CRMs and billing software. We configure everything around the tools you already use.

How do the invoice follow-ups work?

The system watches your connected billing software for overdue invoices, sends scheduled reminders, stops as soon as payment is recorded, and tells your staff when a case needs a personal touch.
