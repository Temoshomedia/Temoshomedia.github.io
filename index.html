The problem

Your customers don't stop asking questions when your business closes.

Every day, businesses deal with:
Repetitive customer enquiries that consume employees' time.
Missed messages outside business hours.
Slow responses that frustrate potential customers.
Support teams spending hours answering the same questions.
Enquiries that get lost in crowded inboxes.
Hiring additional employees isn't always the most practical solution. What if you could delegate much of that repetitive work instead?
The solution

Meet your AI Customer Support Agent.

A digital employee trained on your exact company information, services, FAQs, and customer support guidelines. It works across your customer communication channels to handle routine enquiries and assist customers without requiring someone to manually respond to every message.

What your digital employee can do

01

24/7 Customer Communication

Responds to incoming customer enquiries, even when your office is closed. Customers don't have to wait until the next business day for basic assistance.

02

Trained on Your Company Data

Understands your business information, services, frequently asked questions, policies, and approved responses.

03

Automated Customer Intake

Collects customer names, email addresses, contact details, and enquiry information before passing qualified requests to your team.

04

Email Inbox Management

Monitors incoming enquiries, drafts or sends approved responses, categorises messages, and helps keep your inbox organised.

05

Intelligent Customer Support

Handles common questions, provides relevant information, and escalates sensitive or complicated requests to a human employee.

06

Human Handover

Your team stays in control. When an enquiry requires human attention, the agent can notify the appropriate person and pass along the conversation.

You assign the responsibilities. The agent handles the repetitive work. Your team handles what matters most.
